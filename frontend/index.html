Methodology

  • Toss advisor (shipped): calibrated logistic regression on venue + month + season. 5-fold CV AUC …; post-2021 holdout accuracy ….
  • Live win probability (shipped): two logistic models on 41k innings-states from 260k balls. Chase model test AUC …; 1st-innings …. See Live Predictor tab for the accuracy-by-over curve.
  • Ratings: Elo (K=24, home +30) updated chronologically, plus all-time win %, last-5 form, head-to-head and venue records — all leakage-free.
  • Win predictor (deliberately not shipped): tested 3 models on 872 pre-2022 matches and held out 2022–24. Accuracy stayed at 45–50% — the 2022 mega-auction reset squads and broke every historical feature (Elo alone: 39% in 2022). Pre-toss match-level prediction is a coin flip on this data, so it is analytics + toss intelligence instead of a fake predictor.
  • Data: 1,095 IPL matches (2008–2024), 1,090 decided + 260,920 balls. Squad pools use chronological career stats per (player, team), so auction team-switches are handled with no leakage.
